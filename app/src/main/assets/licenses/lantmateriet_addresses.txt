addresses.txt

Addresses: Lantmäteriet, Belägenhetsadresser (Nedladdning, vektor), CC BY 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The municipalities of Värmlands län and Örebro län.

Only addresses in force or reserved for new buildings: street, village or farm address area,
number and letter, postcode, post town, municipality, point (converted from SWEREF 99 TM to
WGS 84) and the place's popular name. No names of people. The street names it adds to
karlstad_districts.json come from the same source.
